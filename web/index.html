The yard

  • Trunk line with a station board: a place (the repo)
  • Coal heap by the station: grows with the line's tool calls
  • Big engine on the trunk: a session; brass plate: its git branch
  • Small engine on a siding off its lead's ladder: a subagent; lane name at the buffer stop

Signals

  • Green: busy
  • Amber: idle, or a resting subagent
  • Flashing red, bell, red beam: needs you; the red-framed board above says why: "?" for a question, a padlock for a permission prompt
  • Dark, engine cold and grey in the shed: offline

What it is doing

  • edit: freight crates craned onto the wagon
  • run: dark smoke, wheels turning
  • read: an inspector walks the train with a lantern
  • web: telegraph pole, sparks and pulses on the wires
  • delegate: levers throw, pulses run into the sidings
  • think: slow white steam; other: whistle steam from the cab
  • A whistle blast and headlamp flash: a new tool call
  • Red smoke: stalled (a session busy, or a subagent quiet, for over 5 minutes)
